# Stakeholder Memo — Savings Goal Tracker

**To:** Myself (decision-maker & user)
**From:** Kathy Yang
**Date:** 08-24-2026
**Re:** Tracking progress toward a 6-month emergency rent fund

## The Decision This Analysis Supports

Each month, after reviewing my bank statement, I need to answer one question:
**"Am I on track to save $18,000 (6 months × $3,000 rent) within a reasonable
timeframe — and if not, what should I change?"**

This isn't just a curiosity — it directly affects whether I keep my current
spending habits or actively cut back in specific categories next month.

## Who Uses This and When

I am both the person making the decision and the person reviewing the numbers,
since this is a personal (not shared) account. I plan to check this monthly,
right after each new bank statement becomes available — not in real time, since
daily fluctuations aren't meaningful for a 6-month savings goal.

## What "Useful" Looks Like

A dashboard full of every transaction isn't useful to me. What I actually need is:
- **One number:** projected months remaining until I hit $18,000, given my
  current average savings rate.
- **One "what-if":** how much sooner I'd reach the goal if I saved an extra
  $200–500/month.
- **A quick spending breakdown** by category, so I know *where* to cut if I'm
  behind schedule.

## Pain Points This Solves

- Right now I have no clear sense of whether my current habits will actually
  get me to $18,000, or whether I'm drifting off track without realizing it.
- I don't have visibility into which spending categories are eating into my
  savings the most.
- Without a projection, I can't tell if a small lifestyle change now would
  make a meaningful difference, or if I'd need a bigger change.

## Constraints I'm Working With

- Only 3 months of transaction history available so far — projections early
  on will be rougher and should improve as more data accumulates.
- Data comes from a single personal bank account (CSV export), manually
  reviewed monthly — not automated or real-time.
- Bank transaction data is sensitive and will **not** be pushed to GitHub
  (excluded via `.gitignore`).
